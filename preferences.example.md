## Like when
- e.g. sporty / outdoorsy, photos show an actual life (travel, mountains, friends)
- e.g. natural look, smiling in at least one photo

- e.g. only group photos, can't tell who she is

- e.g. smoking in photos

## Neutral, don't decide on these
- e.g. empty bio is fine if photos are good

## Pickiness
- e.g. like roughly 1 in 3. When unsure, pass.
